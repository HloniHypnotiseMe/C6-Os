# C6 Product Company Operating Models

Every product is treated as an operating company, not a flat application.

Embedded manifests:
- C6 Group -> C6-Group-Final-Website/governance/COMPANY-OPERATING-MODEL.yaml
- Ubernie -> ubernie/governance/COMPANY-OPERATING-MODEL.yaml
- RemotePay -> RemotePay-/governance/COMPANY-OPERATING-MODEL.yaml
- DieselConnect -> diesel-connect/governance/COMPANY-OPERATING-MODEL.yaml
- Commodity Connect -> COMMODITY-CONNECT/governance/COMPANY-OPERATING-MODEL.yaml
- Tax & Secretarial -> remote-pay-holdings/governance/COMPANY-OPERATING-MODEL.yaml
- Domains & Hosting -> domains-manager/governance/COMPANY-OPERATING-MODEL.yaml
- Association OS -> c6-association-os/governance/COMPANY-OPERATING-MODEL.yaml
- C6 AI Operations -> C6Group.AiOS/governance/COMPANY-OPERATING-MODEL.yaml

Architecture:
Holding / Board
  -> Product CEO
    -> Department Heads
      -> Specialist Agents
        -> Platform Services / Tools
          -> Evidence + Audit

The CEO and department-head roles are operating identities. They are not evidence of human appointments or regulated authority.
