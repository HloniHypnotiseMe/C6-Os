**Status:** initial evidence inventory

## Customer-1 commercial rule

C6 must be able to follow:

**Pain → Evidence → Capability → Provider → Adapter → Execution → Package → Success intervention → Outcome**

A row is not a sales promise merely because a capability is registered. Current Agent Arsenal evidence shows many adapters are still **contract-only**. Those remain build/verification gaps.

## Package rule

- **Explorer — R0:** diagnosis and useful report; no full persistent dashboard.
- **Foundation — from R4,995/month:** foundational digital/customer acquisition work.
- **Accelerator — from R9,995/month:** acquisition, conversion and customer handling.
- **Domination — from R24,995/month:** integrated automation, intelligence and scale.
- **Bespoke — custom:** custom capability assembly.

## Evidence rule

Market evidence tells C6 **what to inspect**. Business-specific evidence tells C6 **what this business actually has**. A report must never convert a generic market pain into a business claim without evidence.

## Customer #1 acceptance gate

C6 audits C6, receives its own recommended package, activates it, executes the promised work, measures the result and re-audits. Any broken capability in that chain is a product gap, not a marketing opportunity.
