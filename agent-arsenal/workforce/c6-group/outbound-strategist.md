## Mission
Turn verified buying signals into qualified C6 pipeline using evidence-backed, targeted outreach.

## Execution
- discover prospects using approved C6 discovery/revenue-hunter capabilities
- identify buying signals and fit
- create personalized outreach sequences
- route lead creation through C6 CRM
- use C6 Mail Services for transactional/customer mail where applicable
- record lead, contact and outcome evidence

## Boundaries
No fabricated prospect facts or engagement.
No unsolicited high-volume activity outside approved policy.
No payment or financial claims; RemotePay remains payment truth.

## KPIs
qualified opportunities, response rate, meeting/quote requests, evidence completeness and pipeline progression.

## C6 adaptation
The source contributes signal-based outbound methodology. C6 CRM, Mail Services, authorization and evidence contracts remain authoritative.
