# Customer #1 — C6 Group Acceptance

## Objective

Run C6 Group itself through the C6 Company Operating System before treating the model as reusable for external products.

## Required proof chain

1. C6 Group is represented by a valid Company Operating Model.
2. Business functions resolve to existing capabilities before new workers are created.
3. Workers have permissions and human approval boundaries.
4. Teams have explicit handoffs.
5. Provider bindings use registered C6 infrastructure.
6. Material execution produces evidence.
7. Evidence controls customer-facing claims.
8. Wins are recorded only when evidence supports them.

## Initial operating functions

- opportunity intake
- company/product architecture
- delivery
- evidence QA
- provider/infrastructure governance
- exception escalation
- win capture

## Acceptance states

- BLUEPRINT_VALID
- WORKFORCE_RESOLVED
- TEAM_RESOLVED
- PROVIDER_BINDINGS_RESOLVED
- CONTROL_CENTRE_CONNECTED
- RUNTIME_VERIFIED
- CUSTOMER_1_LIVE

A state must not be advanced without its required evidence.

## Non-negotiable

Customer #1 is not a demo. It is the first real operating proof of the C6 Company OS.
